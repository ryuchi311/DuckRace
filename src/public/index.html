LEGAL & PRIVACY POLICIES

COPPA • GDPR / Privacy • DMCA • Subscriptions & Email Terms

Children's Online Privacy Protection Act (COPPA) Notice: Ultimate Duck Race is intended solely for general audience entertainment and is NOT directed to children under 13 years of age. We do not knowingly collect, request, or maintain personal identifiable information from children under 13.

1. Age Gate & Participant Safeguards

Any participant joining a race stage via phone or online controller must explicitly certify that they are at least 13 years of age. If you are under 13, you are strictly prohibited from submitting your name, battle cries, or personal information.

2. Parental Notice & Deletion Requests

If a parent or guardian discovers that their child under 13 has submitted their name or personal data without parental consent, please contact our team immediately at privacy@duckrace.local. Upon notification, we will immediately and permanently delete any corresponding records from our system and database.

Visitor Privacy & Third-Party Disclosure (GDPR / CCPA) We respect your privacy. Ultimate Duck Race operates primarily locally and peer-to-peer. We do not sell, rent, or trade your personal data.

1. What Data We Process

  • Racer Names & Battle Cries: Stored locally in your browser (localStorage) and temporarily in memory during live race sessions.
  • Room PINs: Randomly generated strings used to coordinate stage sessions.
  • Optional Cloud Sync: If enabled by your host configuration, stage names and cheer signals are transmitted to Firebase Firestore or PeerJS servers.

2. Third-Party Service Providers

We do not load third-party cloud sync or external API calls without your explicit consent. You can view and manage your privacy choices at any time using the Cookie & Cloud Consent settings below.

Digital Millennium Copyright Act (DMCA) Notice & Policy We respect the intellectual property rights of creators and expect users to do the same. All character duck vector artworks and engine sounds are original or licensed assets.

1. User-Submitted Content & Custom Names

Users may input custom duck names and battle cries during race sessions. Users agree not to input copyrighted trademarks or infringing materials. We maintain a zero-tolerance policy for repeat infringers and reserve the right to ban or remove any offending participant ID or room.

2. Filing a Copyright Takedown Notice

If you believe your copyrighted work has been used in a manner that constitutes infringement, please submit a DMCA Takedown Notice to our Designated Copyright Agent with:

  1. Identification of the copyrighted work claimed to have been infringed.
  2. Identification of the material claimed to be infringing (including Room PIN, participant name, or screenshot).
  3. Your contact details: Name, physical address, telephone number, and email address.
  4. A statement that you have a good faith belief that the disputed use is not authorized by the copyright owner.
  5. A statement under penalty of perjury that the information is accurate and that you are authorized to act on behalf of the owner.

HOW TO PLAY DUCK RACE

Interactive Guide & Feature Walkthrough

💡 Duck Race is an exciting physics simulation where up to 100 unique ducks swim down a raging river through rapids, whirlpools, bread boosts, and photo finishes!

⚡ The 3-Step Simple Flow

1

Customize Racers

Use the Bulk Name Importer to paste names (e.g. classroom, team, or party members) or pick from 24 funny duck archetypes.

2

Set Rules & Theme

Pick a sprint duration (10s, 20s, or 40s) and select your environment map: Rapids, Ocean, Cyber Stream, or Lava Moat!

3

Watch & Cheer!

Hit START RACE. Tap or click any duck to cheer for them and trigger an instant speed surge!

Want to test it instantly?

📋 Managing Ducks & Assigning Names

🏷️ Bulk Name Importer (Fastest Way) Paste a list of names into the big box on the right. Hit "✨ Apply & Match Fleet" to automatically enable exactly that number of ducks and assign the names in order!
🎲 Quick Fleet Presets Click "🦆 100" for a grand 100-duck championship, "🎲 25" for a quick party round, or "✅ All" to toggle racers.
✏️ Individual Card Customization Click directly on any duck card in the roster grid to change their name, toggle active status, or pick their victory battle cry!

🏁 Live Race Interactive Controls

👆 Click/Tap Any Duck

Tapping a duck gives them a Cheer Boost with custom sound effects and locks the broadcast camera onto them!

⚡ Quack Surge (Spacebar)

Spawns instant boost pads, bread crumbs, and whirlpool currents to shake up the standings!

🎥 10 Broadcast Camera POVs

Switch between Leader Pan, Duck-Eye GoPro, 3D Kart Chase, Drone, Helicopter Orbit, Underdog Cam, and Split-Screen!

🏆 Results & Export

At the finish line, watch the grand podium ceremony, export full standings to CSV or TXT, or remove 1st place to race again.

📱 Hosting for Live Audiences & Friends

1. Click "📡 Host IRL QR Race" This empties the starting line and shows a big scannable QR code on the live track.
2. Audience Scans with Phone Camera Participants can scan the QR code (or enter the Room PIN) on their phones. They pick their character duck, type their name, and appear on the starting gate!
3. Live Mobile Gamepad & Cheers During the race, audience phones turn into interactive controllers with a big QUACK BOOST button to cheer their duck live!
